# Canonical LO Box 1 Five-Surface Projection Contract V1

Contract ID: `loan-originator-box-1-projection-v1`  
Status: canonical projection authority; not runtime implementation authority.

## Locked visible-surface identity

The five Founder/LO Spock locked customer-facing surface labels and order are exact:

1. **Your Business Now**
2. **Where Your Business Comes From**
3. **Your Business Pipeline**
4. **Your System & Capacity**
5. **What’s Holding You Back**

Internal evidence missions may use normalized identifiers, but each maps one-to-one to exactly one of these visible surfaces. The mapping may not collapse the dedicated business-source surface into Pipeline, split Systems from Capacity into separate visible surfaces, or rename the final diagnosis surface.

## Shared projection envelope

Every surface receives:

- surface status (`FULL`, `NARROW`, `CONTRADICTORY`, `ABSTAIN`, `ON_TRACK`, `OUT_OF_SCOPE` as applicable);
- headline/customer recognition statement;
- trailing-12-month baseline facts;
- trailing-90-day trajectory facts where compatible;
- relevant goal comparison;
- evidence IDs/classes/provenance;
- confidence and applicability;
- contradictions/missingness and next evidence;
- control-owner/authority qualifiers;
- no hidden benchmark/scenario promotion.

Baseline and trajectory are embedded within the five surfaces. No sixth box is created.

## Surface 1 — Your Business Now

### Truth payload

- confirmed producing role/business variant and subject scope;
- funded units/volume trailing 12 months;
- funded units/volume trailing 90 days where compatible;
- Purchase/Refinance/other applicability and mix;
- primary goal and forward 12-month scope;
- derived pace/trajectory only under compatible definitions;
- economic/current-goal view only when applicable.

### Customer behavior

- Strong: concise baseline, trajectory, purpose mix, and goal relationship.
- Sparse: show known production/goal and name missing period/definition/trajectory.
- Contradictory: show both incompatible claims and no false trend/backsolve.
- On Track: recognize current alignment without manufacturing deficiency.
- Out of scope: no LO surface.

## Surface 2 — Where Your Business Comes From

### Truth payload

- OSN and CRN visibly separate;
- source/customer-network mix and attributable outcomes;
- raw meaningful/active/productive measures with windows;
- diversification/concentration;
- relationship-system links;
- contact/use/servicing/recapture authority qualifier where materially relevant.

### Customer behavior

- Never imply Realtor primacy.
- Past-customer referral provenance remains CRN.
- Contact count alone is not relationship health/equity.
- Missing authority narrows action conclusions, not all network recognition.
- No CRM audit or borrower list.

## Surface 3 — Your Business Pipeline

### Truth payload

- applicable Purchase and/or Refinance event path;
- explicit event definitions, periods, cohorts, and pending share;
- Opportunity/Application/funded counts and intermediate stages where known;
- derived compatible conversion/pull-through/fallout;
- cycle/aging/reason evidence when Conditional 13 was material;
- modeled requirements/backsolve inputs with sensitivity.

### Customer behavior

- `NOT_APPLICABLE`, zero, missing, and contradiction stay distinct.
- Incompatible denominators do not produce ratios.
- Open/pending outcomes do not silently become fallout.
- Strong case highlights the actual pipeline machine; sparse case localizes abstention.

## Surface 4 — Your System & Capacity

### Truth payload

- measured OSN/CRN and operating systems;
- tools separated from systems;
- who/what/how often/how delivered/owner/measure/outcome;
- team/support/work ownership and handoffs;
- workload, sustainable capacity, backlog/service effect;
- platform/company capability limits and control owners;
- execution/accountability evidence.

### Customer behavior

- No absent support field becomes “solo.”
- No processor-first recommendation.
- Platform/shared/external constraints remain attributed.
- Missing system measures show “not yet measured,” not zero execution.

## Surface 5 — What’s Holding You Back

### Truth payload

- one of four communication states;
- primary constraint only when evidence clears the gate;
- secondary constraints/risks;
- operator hypothesis with attributed status;
- evidence/confidence/alternatives/control owner;
- missing/contradictory evidence and exact next evidence;
- On Track recognition or no-primary-constraint behavior.

### Customer behavior

- **Opportunity Gap:** current compatible flow below modeled requirement.
- **Opportunity Sufficient / Other Constraint:** flow appears sufficient and another supported constraint exists.
- **On Track:** no material supported limiter; protect/compound/continue may matter more than repair.
- **Insufficient Evidence:** say exactly what is known/unknown and abstain locally.

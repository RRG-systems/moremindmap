# Canonical LO Opportunity Sufficiency + Constraint Contract V1

Authority ID: `loan-originator-sufficiency-constraint-v1`

## Four communication states

### Opportunity Gap

Use only when compatible current mature Opportunity flow appears below the modeled requirement for the governed goal. Required evidence: goal, current flow, compatible supported rates/model, assumptions, feasibility, and confidence.

### Opportunity Sufficient / Other Constraint

Use when compatible flow appears sufficient and a better-supported downstream/other constraint exists. Do not auto-scale. Test conversion, pull-through, execution/systems, capacity, platform, economics, concentration, and evidence maturity.

### On Track

Use when current evidence is broadly consistent with the goal and no material intervention constraint is supported. Preserve risks/missingness without manufacturing a problem.

### Insufficient Evidence

State exactly what is known, what cannot be determined, which evidence is missing/contradictory, and what evidence would resolve the material uncertainty. Do not hide behind a generic confidence label.

## Constraint families

| Family | Supporting evidence | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opportunity Supply | compatible required vs current flow; source mix/productivity/concentration | no Realtor or lead-generation default |
| Relationship Activation | OSN/CRN system, engagement, attributable outcomes | contact count/tool ownership insufficient |
| Conversion | compatible Opportunity→Application or other early-stage drop | explanation remains hypothesis unless supported |
| Pull-Through / Fallout | compatible later-stage ratios/outcomes/cycle | open/pending cohort is not fallout |
| Execution / Systems | defined system, target, actual execution, outcome/learning | no moral judgment; strategy and execution distinct |
| Capacity / Leverage | workload, sustainable capacity, service effect, work ownership | add leverage at constraint; no processor-first rule |
| Platform / Capability | material platform limit, business effect, control owner | no employer/recruiting recommendation |
| Economics / Business Model | applicable economic scope, goal gap, contribution/capacity | no assumed compensation/personal finance |

## Primary constraint decision

One primary constraint may be asserted only when all are true:

1. governed goal exists;
2. evaluated constraint is material to that goal;
3. compatible evidence supports the mechanism;
4. reasonable alternatives were considered;
5. control owner/dependency is visible;
6. confidence is sufficient for a responsible statement;
7. no unresolved contradiction invalidates the comparison;
8. the constraint is more decision-useful than abstention.

Primary does not mean sole cause. Secondary constraints and risks remain visible. If no evaluated constraint clears the gate, the result is no-primary-constraint with On Track or Insufficient Evidence as appropriate.

## Causality discipline

- Customer/operator belief is an attributed hypothesis.
- Association and timing do not prove business cause.
- Personality never establishes business cause.
- Platform/shared/external dependencies stay separated.
- A modeled requirement does not prove a constraint.
- A benchmark gap does not prove customer underperformance.
- Current external context may qualify feasibility but does not rewrite observed business truth.

## BA versus coaching

BA recognizes and diagnoses the governed state. It does not prescribe a deterministic intervention. Futures, One Move, Plan, Evidence, and Subscription reason over the governed projection.

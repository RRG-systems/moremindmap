# Canonical LO Opportunity Source Network + Customer Relationship Network Contract V1

Authority ID: `loan-originator-osn-crn-authority-v1`

## Two distinct governed objects

| Object | What it is | What it is not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opportunity Source Network | professional/business source relationships and applicable channels that repeatedly create mortgage Opportunities | a list of all contacts; a Realtor-only database; the borrower funnel itself |
| Customer Relationship Network | past/current customer relationships that may create repeat Purchase, Refinance, or friends/family referral Opportunities | automatic servicing/recapture/contact authority; OSN merely because a referral occurred |
| Opportunity | an identifiable person/household with a plausible current mortgage need | the source relationship, CRM row, email recipient, impression, or unqualified purchased name |

## OSN row grammar

Each material OSN row may contain:

- stable row ID and customer label;
- source type and relationship/channel distinction;
- LO/shared/platform/external control owner;
- meaningful size or channel scope;
- raw active/engaged measure + definition/window;
- raw productive measure + definition/window;
- attributable Opportunity, Application, and funded outcomes for trailing 12 months and optional 90 days;
- source activity/system link;
- concentration and mix;
- attribution quality and contradiction state;
- purpose applicability.

No universal active/productive threshold is frozen. The product displays raw definitions and windows until future expert/Founder authority establishes a safe threshold.

## CRN grammar

CRN aggregate evidence may contain:

- meaningful aggregate network size and categories;
- maintained/active relationship state under customer definition/window;
- repeat Purchase, Refinance, and friends/family referral Opportunities/outcomes;
- system link and aggregate cadence/outcome;
- contact/use authority state;
- company/LO/shared/third-party data ownership state;
- servicing ownership state;
- recapture authority state;
- source/date/provenance for every authority claim;
- explicit `MISSING`, `UNKNOWN`, `NOT_AUTHORIZED`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE`.

No borrower name, loan-level record, communication body, loan number, underwriting file, or protected-class data is required.

## Cross-reference rule

When a past customer refers another person:

1. the relationship provenance remains `CRN`;
2. the new person/household may become an Opportunity only when the Opportunity definition is satisfied;
3. the Opportunity may be cross-referenced into the funnel/source-channel event;
4. that cross-reference does not reclassify the past customer as OSN;
5. source/outcome counts must avoid double counting.

## Relationship quality

Contact count alone is descriptive. Relationship quality may be hypothesized only through observable proxies such as:

- current two-way engagement;
- relevance/fit;
- observed Opportunity/funded outcomes;
- reliability and concentration;
- repeat/referral behavior;
- system cadence/channel/depth/segmentation;
- attribution quality;
- lawful authority to act.

Trust, strategic fit, and relationship depth remain customer-stated or modeled hypotheses unless governed evidence supports them.

## Control and authority rules

- CRM possession does not prove ownership, consent, contact authority, servicing, or recapture.
- Originator, lender/company, servicer, and recapture owner may differ.
- Platform/company-provided Opportunities are valid OSN/channel rows but their control owner remains platform/shared.
- Platform-fit evidence may influence constraint/feasibility but cannot become Recruiting or employer advice.
- Contact/marketing/referral/compensation action requires applicable company/compliance/legal authority beyond this business assessment.

## Box 1 projection rules

Where Your Business Comes From may show:

- OSN and CRN as visibly separate portfolios;
- source/customer contribution and mix;
- diversification/concentration;
- productivity only from compatible aggregate evidence;
- measured relationship systems;
- narrow/unknown authority flags when they materially qualify an interpretation.

It must not show a giant CRM inventory, borrower rows, or a numeric “relationship equity” score unsupported by evidence.

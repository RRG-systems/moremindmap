## 1. Mission

Identify the system constraint governing goal attainment and distinguish leverage from local optimization.

## 2. Scope

- throughput flow
- physical, policy, market, skill, decision and capital constraints
- queues and starvation
- subordination
- constraint migration
- bottleneck versus broad weakness
- feedback delays

## 3. Non-Scope

- Current market, law, regulation, rate, platform, competitor and benchmark facts belong to Dynamic Research.
- Outcome claims about what intervention works for whom belong to future RSL after execution/outcome evidence.
- Whole-Person Authority may explain implementation friction or fit; it cannot establish business facts.
- Customer report choreography, required wording, Five Futures runtime math and production integration are outside this Bible.

## 4. Core Primitives

- system goal
- throughput
- inventory/work in process
- operating expense
- constraint
- bottleneck
- queue
- starvation
- utilization
- subordination
- elevation
- constraint migration

## 5. Causal Mechanisms

- System throughput is limited by the active constraint.
- Improving non-constraints can create inventory, rework or idle capacity without changing output.
- Maximizing utilization everywhere increases coordination and queue cost.
- Founder approvals and scarce senior judgment are frequent invisible constraints in knowledge work.
- After elevation, the constraint moves and must be re-identified.

## 6. Evidence Inputs

- flow and stage timestamps
- queues/backlogs and handoffs
- capacity by stage
- lost demand and wait time
- rework and quality failures
- decision/approval latency
- resource schedules
- customer-facing lead times

Evidence must retain source identity, date/period, units where relevant, provenance class, confidence, contradictions and missingness. Customer-stated evidence is not automatically verified; model inference is not observation.

## 7. Diagnostic Questions

- Where does work accumulate and downstream work starve?
- What extra unit of capacity would raise system throughput?
- Is the claimed constraint active at the current demand level?
- What is being optimized that is not the constraint?
- What policy or decision right creates hidden scarcity?
- What changes after the constraint is elevated?

## 8. Strong Patterns

- One limiting flow point is evidenced.
- Non-constraints subordinate schedules and work release.
- Constraint time is protected from avoidable work/rework.
- Measures follow throughput rather than local busyness.

Strong patterns are diagnostic hypotheses supported by evidence, not templates that force every business into the same conclusion.

## 9. Failure Modes

- Every problem is called a constraint.
- Symptoms are attacked downstream of the cause.
- More leads are acquired into a conversion/capacity bottleneck.
- Automation speeds a non-constraint.
- The founder remains an approval queue.

## 10. Counterexamples and Confounds

- Several near-equal constraints can create a constraint set.
- A temporary shock may imitate a persistent bottleneck.
- Demand scarcity and internal capacity weakness can coexist.

## 12. Business-Model Differences

- Manufacturing often reveals physical queues.
- Services reveal calendar, skill and senior-review constraints.
- SaaS may be constrained by demand, activation, onboarding or support.
- Recruiting/sales businesses may be constrained by candidate/demand quality or conversion.

## 17. Evidence, Confidence and Uncertainty

- `OBSERVED`: directly supported by governed current evidence.
- `CUSTOMER_STATED`: attributed to the customer, not independently verified.
- `DERIVED`: formula or deterministic transformation with inputs/units/formula present.
- `SUPPORTED_HYPOTHESIS`: mechanism fits evidence but alternatives remain.
- `FOUNDER_HEURISTIC`: useful operating doctrine, qualified and parameterized.
- `DYNAMIC_CURRENT`: externally sourced current fact with retrieval date.
- `UNKNOWN`: missing, stale, contradictory or not safely inferable.

Confidence must be claim-specific. Missing or conflicting evidence survives into state; it is never replaced by a target, benchmark, generic profile value or provider prose.

## 18. Falsifiers

- The hypothesized mechanism is weakened when the named outcome changes while its alleged driver remains unchanged under comparable conditions.
- A competing mechanism explains the same evidence better and survives its own confound tests.
- Source lineage, period, units or identity do not support the claim.
- High execution fidelity produces no movement in the named leading indicators over the stated horizon, absent material confounds.
- New verified evidence contradicts the state contribution.

## 19. Intervention Relevance

- exploit current constraint
- subordinate work release and non-constraints
- reduce rework at constraint
- change decision rights
- elevate capacity only after exploitation
- instrument flow and reassess

This Bible may identify mechanisms, candidate movers, observability, reversibility, adoption burdens and evidence needed. It does not prescribe customer wording, claim causal effect, or determine One Move alone.

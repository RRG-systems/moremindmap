## 1. Mission

Model how work and judgment are multiplied through people, roles, systems, capital and decision architecture without multiplying coordination cost or defects.

## 2. Scope

- role/seat and work design
- span and layers
- decision rights
- delegation and transfer of judgment
- founder dependence
- coordination load
- team capacity
- leverage economics
- succession/transferability

## 5. Causal Mechanisms

- Adding people without stable work/rights multiplies ambiguity and rework.
- Delegating tasks without authority or context preserves founder bottlenecks.
- Coordination load can grow faster than productive capacity.
- Systems and role clarity make leverage possible; people alone do not.
- Role fit affects execution, but structural causes must be tested before personality explanations.

## 6. Evidence Inputs

- work/role inventory
- decision and approval maps
- capacity/load by role
- handoffs/escalations
- manager span and meeting load
- founder-owned recurring decisions
- team output/margin
- turnover/ramp
- documented systems and succession risk

Evidence must retain source identity, date/period, units where relevant, provenance class, confidence, contradictions and missingness. Customer-stated evidence is not automatically verified; model inference is not observation.

## 7. Diagnostic Questions

- What work/judgment is trapped in one person?
- Does each role own an outcome and authority?
- What breaks when a key person is absent?
- Does the next hire remove the constraint or add coordination?
- Can the business transfer customer trust/data/process?
- Is a people problem actually workload, structure, incentive or system?

## 8. Strong Patterns

- Roles follow value/flow rather than titles.
- Authority follows accountability.
- Scarce judgment is codified/transferred.
- Team capacity and economics improve after leverage.

Strong patterns are diagnostic hypotheses supported by evidence, not templates that force every business into the same conclusion.

## 9. Failure Modes

- Hire before model/process clarity.
- Founder delegates work but retains decisions.
- Role overlap and rescue behavior.
- Team headcount rises while throughput/margin falls.
- Personality is blamed for structural ambiguity.

## 10. Counterexamples and Confounds

- Early small teams legitimately use broad roles.
- Expert services may retain deliberate senior involvement.
- Short-term overlap may be rational during transition.

- Services leverage through junior work, systems and senior review.
- SaaS leverage includes software, support and customer success.
- Local service uses crews/routes/supervision.
- Sales teams need lead ownership, credit and manager economics.

## 15. Whole-Person Interaction Boundary

Use the frozen Whole-Person Model selectively to understand adoption, attention, decision, communication, pressure, leadership, role or implementation mechanics. ## 16. Team and Organization Boundary

Retrieve only relevant people by business/team membership, role, reporting relationship, permission and timestamp. Test structure, incentives, workload, role design, authority, capacity and missing systems before personality. Never average people into a team personality.

## 17. Evidence, Confidence and Uncertainty

- `OBSERVED`: directly supported by governed current evidence.
- `CUSTOMER_STATED`: attributed to the customer, not independently verified.
- `DERIVED`: formula or deterministic transformation with inputs/units/formula present.
- `SUPPORTED_HYPOTHESIS`: mechanism fits evidence but alternatives remain.
- `FOUNDER_HEURISTIC`: useful operating doctrine, qualified and parameterized.
- `DYNAMIC_CURRENT`: externally sourced current fact with retrieval date.
- `UNKNOWN`: missing, stale, contradictory or not safely inferable.

Confidence must be claim-specific. Missing or conflicting evidence survives into state; it is never replaced by a target, benchmark, generic profile value or provider prose.

## 18. Falsifiers

- The hypothesized mechanism is weakened when the named outcome changes while its alleged driver remains unchanged under comparable conditions.
- A competing mechanism explains the same evidence better and survives its own confound tests.
- Source lineage, period, units or identity do not support the claim.
- High execution fidelity produces no movement in the named leading indicators over the stated horizon, absent material confounds.
- New verified evidence contradicts the state contribution.

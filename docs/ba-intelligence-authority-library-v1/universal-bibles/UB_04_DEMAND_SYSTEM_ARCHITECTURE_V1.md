## 1. Mission

Explain how demand is created, captured, owned, qualified, diversified and renewed as an economic system.

## 2. Scope

- outbound
- paid/direct response
- organic/audience
- partnership/channel

## 5. Causal Mechanisms

- Reach without capture does not build an owned demand asset.
- Rented channels can scale but expose the business to price/platform change.
- Relationship assets compound only when identity, consent, recency and useful contact are maintained.
- Source quality changes conversion, service burden, economics and lifetime value.
- Diversification reduces fragility but too many weak lanes dilute execution.

## 6. Evidence Inputs

- source-attributed opportunities
- owned contact/database records
- capture and qualification rates
- source-specific conversion and economics
- recency/touch/nurture history
- channel concentration and dependency
- time-to-conversion
- lost-demand reasons

Evidence must retain source identity, date/period, units where relevant, provenance class, confidence, contradictions and missingness. Customer-stated evidence is not automatically verified; model inference is not observation.

## 7. Diagnostic Questions

- Where does opportunity originate?
- Who owns access to the audience/contact?
- What percentage is captured, qualified and followed?
- Which source produces value after conversion and delivery cost?
- Is weak production a demand problem or a conversion/capacity problem?
- What channel loss would materially impair the business?

## 8. Strong Patterns

- A small set of executable lanes with clear economics.
- Owned relationship/contact assets grow alongside rented reach.
- Capture and follow-up are designed, not assumed.
- Channel quality and concentration are explicit.

Strong patterns are diagnostic hypotheses supported by evidence, not templates that force every business into the same conclusion.

## 9. Failure Modes

- More volume is prescribed into a conversion bottleneck.
- Followers/impressions are treated as owned demand.
- Database size is treated as relationship quality.
- Every possible channel is attempted.
- Attribution credits the last touch only.

## 10. Counterexamples and Confounds

- Concentration can be rational when advantages are durable and contingency exists.
- A young channel may lack lagged outcome data.
- Brand activity may assist sources not captured by last-touch attribution.

- Wholesale/channel businesses depend on partner activation and share of wallet.

Use the frozen Whole-Person Model selectively to understand adoption, attention, decision, communication, pressure, leadership, role or implementation mechanics. ## 16. Team and Organization Boundary

Retrieve only relevant people by business/team membership, role, reporting relationship, permission and timestamp. Test structure, incentives, workload, role design, authority, capacity and missing systems before personality. Never average people into a team personality.

## 17. Evidence, Confidence and Uncertainty

- `OBSERVED`: directly supported by governed current evidence.
- `CUSTOMER_STATED`: attributed to the customer, not independently verified.
- `DERIVED`: formula or deterministic transformation with inputs/units/formula present.
- `SUPPORTED_HYPOTHESIS`: mechanism fits evidence but alternatives remain.
- `FOUNDER_HEURISTIC`: useful operating doctrine, qualified and parameterized.
- `DYNAMIC_CURRENT`: externally sourced current fact with retrieval date.
- `UNKNOWN`: missing, stale, contradictory or not safely inferable.

Confidence must be claim-specific. Missing or conflicting evidence survives into state; it is never replaced by a target, benchmark, generic profile value or provider prose.

## 18. Falsifiers

- The hypothesized mechanism is weakened when the named outcome changes while its alleged driver remains unchanged under comparable conditions.
- A competing mechanism explains the same evidence better and survives its own confound tests.
- Source lineage, period, units or identity do not support the claim.
- High execution fidelity produces no movement in the named leading indicators over the stated horizon, absent material confounds.
- New verified evidence contradicts the state contribution.

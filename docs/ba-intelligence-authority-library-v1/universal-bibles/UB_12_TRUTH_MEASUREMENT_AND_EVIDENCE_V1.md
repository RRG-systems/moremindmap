## 1. Mission

Govern what the business knows, believes and infers; make evidence, uncertainty, contradiction, causality and learning explicit.

## 2. Scope

- metric definitions and lineage
- leading/lagging indicators
- evidence quality/confidence
- cohorts/baselines
- attribution and causal hypotheses
- contradictions/falsifiers
- experiments and intervention fidelity
- state updates and missing evidence

## 3. Non-Scope

- Current market, law, regulation, rate, platform, competitor and benchmark facts belong to Dynamic Research.
- Outcome claims about what intervention works for whom belong to future RSL after execution/outcome evidence.
- Whole-Person Authority may explain implementation friction or fit; it cannot establish business facts.
- Customer report choreography, required wording, Five Futures runtime math and production integration are outside this Bible.

## 5. Causal Mechanisms

- Invalid metrics create confident but wrong allocation.
- Lagging-only measurement delays correction.
- Attribution error rewards the wrong mechanism.
- Strategy maps are causal hypotheses, not permanent truth.
- New evidence should update state rather than append narrative.
- An outcome cannot evaluate an intervention without execution fidelity, horizon and confounds.

## 6. Evidence Inputs

- source/identity/version/timestamp
- metric definition and units
- completeness/recency
- repeated observations/cohorts
- contradictory evidence
- experiment design and baseline
- execution/fidelity records
- outcomes, confounds and external shocks

Evidence must retain source identity, date/period, units where relevant, provenance class, confidence, contradictions and missingness. Customer-stated evidence is not automatically verified; model inference is not observation.

## 7. Diagnostic Questions

- What do we know versus believe or infer?
- What evidence and lineage support the claim?
- What would change our mind?
- Does the metric predict or cause something important?
- What evidence is missing but obtainable?
- Was the intervention executed well enough to learn?

## 8. Strong Patterns

- Claims carry source, confidence and falsifier.
- Few measures reflect real mechanisms.
- Contradictions remain visible.
- Learning distinguishes outcome, attribution and execution.

Strong patterns are diagnostic hypotheses supported by evidence, not templates that force every business into the same conclusion.

## 9. Failure Modes

- Dashboard color is treated as causal proof.
- Metric definitions drift.
- Vanity/activity measures replace outcomes.
- Small anecdotes become universal doctrine.
- Provider prose becomes upstream evidence.
- Missing data is silently imputed.

## 10. Counterexamples and Confounds

- Sparse data can still support bounded qualitative reasoning.
- External research may improve a claim without becoming canon.
- Controlled experiments may be impractical; observational evidence needs qualification.

## 11. Cross-Authority Interactions

See the Universal cross-authority interaction registry; router should broaden retrieval when another mechanism plausibly controls the outcome.

The authority must preserve upstream claim/evidence references when another authority changes the interpretation. A cross-authority interaction is a hypothesis until the required state and confound evidence are present.

## 12. Business-Model Differences

- Metrics vary: subscription retention, service utilization, transactional conversion, marketplace liquidity, local service route/crew outcomes.
- Universal evidence doctrine is stable while measures are model/vertical specific.

## 13. Vertical Boundary

Residential real-estate workflows, economics, regulation and benchmarks belong in the active vertical cassette.

## 14. Dynamic External-Research Triggers

Invoke bounded current research when the conclusion materially depends on:

- current regulation, market conditions, platform rules and volatile benchmarks

Dynamic findings remain separately dated and sourced; they do not rewrite this frozen Bible.

## 15. Whole-Person Interaction Boundary

Use the frozen Whole-Person Model selectively to understand adoption, attention, decision, communication, pressure, leadership, role or implementation mechanics. Never convert a trait into a business cause without business evidence. Never override financial, operational, market or customer reality. Preserve person, business and relationship provenance separately.

## 16. Team and Organization Boundary

Retrieve only relevant people by business/team membership, role, reporting relationship, permission and timestamp. Test structure, incentives, workload, role design, authority, capacity and missing systems before personality. Never average people into a team personality.

## 17. Evidence, Confidence and Uncertainty

- `OBSERVED`: directly supported by governed current evidence.
- `CUSTOMER_STATED`: attributed to the customer, not independently verified.
- `DERIVED`: formula or deterministic transformation with inputs/units/formula present.
- `SUPPORTED_HYPOTHESIS`: mechanism fits evidence but alternatives remain.
- `FOUNDER_HEURISTIC`: useful operating doctrine, qualified and parameterized.
- `DYNAMIC_CURRENT`: externally sourced current fact with retrieval date.
- `UNKNOWN`: missing, stale, contradictory or not safely inferable.

Confidence must be claim-specific. Missing or conflicting evidence survives into state; it is never replaced by a target, benchmark, generic profile value or provider prose.

## 18. Falsifiers

- The hypothesized mechanism is weakened when the named outcome changes while its alleged driver remains unchanged under comparable conditions.
- A competing mechanism explains the same evidence better and survives its own confound tests.
- Source lineage, period, units or identity do not support the claim.
- High execution fidelity produces no movement in the named leading indicators over the stated horizon, absent material confounds.
- New verified evidence contradicts the state contribution.

## 19. Intervention Relevance

- define/repair metric lineage
- instrument leading indicators
- preserve contradictions/missingness
- bounded experiment
- separate execution from strategy
- update governed state
- route current facts to Dynamic Research

This Bible may identify mechanisms, candidate movers, observability, reversibility, adoption burdens and evidence needed. It does not prescribe customer wording, claim causal effect, or determine One Move alone.

## 22. Open Questions and Known Gaps

- Evidence aggregation without false precision.
- Practical causal learning for small samples.

Open questions do not block bounded reasoning; they lower confidence, trigger evidence collection or route current research. They may not be filled by invention.

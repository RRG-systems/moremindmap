## 1. Mission

Determine whether a real-estate team creates productive capacity and owner profit through clear seats, lead/data ownership, routing, standards, management and economics.

## 2. Scope

- team model and seats
- leader/agent/admin/ISA/TC roles
- lead routing/ownership

- agent productivity

## 5. Causal Mechanisms

- Team leads have no value without agent response/conversion.
- People added before systems/standards create coordination and margin loss.
- Leader rescue hides weak seats and reduces transferability.
- Volume can rise while team profit falls.
- Ambiguous data/relationship ownership creates conflict and succession risk.

## 6. Evidence Inputs

- org/role/decision maps
- lead routing/response/conversion by agent
- compensation/splits/costs
- agent ramp/productivity/retention
- leader production/rescue work
- team P&L excluding leader
- database/relationship ownership

Evidence must retain source identity, date/period, units where relevant, provenance class, confidence, contradictions and missingness. Customer-stated evidence is not automatically verified; model inference is not observation.

## 7. Diagnostic Questions

- What outcome and authority does each seat own?
- Who owns leads, client relationship and database?
- What is productivity/margin by agent/source?
- What recurring work/judgment remains with leader?
- Does the team profit without leader production?

## 8. Strong Patterns

- Roles/standards/economics explicit.
- Lead routing and conversion are measurable.
- Leader dependence declines.

Strong patterns are diagnostic hypotheses supported by evidence, not templates that force every business into the same conclusion.

## 9. Failure Modes

- Personality explains structural ambiguity.
- Team volume substitutes for owner profit.
- Splits ignore fully loaded support/lead cost.
- Low productivity is solved by more recruiting.

## 10. Counterexamples and Confounds

- New agents need bounded ramp investment.
- Leader production can be intentional in boutique team.

## 17. Evidence, Confidence and Uncertainty

- `OBSERVED`: directly supported by governed current evidence.
- `CUSTOMER_STATED`: attributed to the customer, not independently verified.
- `DERIVED`: formula or deterministic transformation with inputs/units/formula present.

## 18. Falsifiers

- The hypothesized mechanism is weakened when the named outcome changes while its alleged driver remains unchanged under comparable conditions.
- A competing mechanism explains the same evidence better and survives its own confound tests.
- Source lineage, period, units or identity do not support the claim.
- High execution fidelity produces no movement in the named leading indicators over the stated horizon, absent material confounds.
- New verified evidence contradicts the state contribution.

## 1. Mission

Translate commissions into durable owner economics, reserves, investment capacity and responsible wealth transition without confusing GCI with income or giving regulated financial advice.

- business/transaction/lead/team costs

## 5. Causal Mechanisms

- Gross commission can mask low net/profit.
- Variable commissions plus recurring team/marketing costs create volatility.
- Hiring without contribution/cash visibility can turn production into fragility.
- Profit allocation and reserves create optionality; lifestyle inflation does not.
- Wealth guidance crosses regulatory boundaries and requires licensed professionals.

## 6. Evidence Inputs

- commission statements
- brokerage/team fees/splits
- business expenses by function/source
- owner draws/payroll/profit
- cash/reserves/tax obligations
- profit per closing/agent/source
- investment/hiring decisions/outcomes

Evidence must retain source identity, date/period, units where relevant, provenance class, confidence, contradictions and missingness. Customer-stated evidence is not automatically verified; model inference is not observation.

## 7. Diagnostic Questions

- What remains from GCI after all costs and owner labor?
- Which source/side/agent produces profit?
- What reserve protects the operating model?
- What evidence supports the next hire/investment?
- Where does business education end and licensed advice begin?

## 8. Strong Patterns

- GCI/net/profit separated.
- Owner compensation and true profit visible.
- Hiring thresholds use contribution/cash/capacity.
- Wealth discussion is bounded/referral-based.

Strong patterns are diagnostic hypotheses supported by evidence, not templates that force every business into the same conclusion.

## 9. Failure Modes

- GCI equals income.
- Tax estimates or investment recommendations are asserted without authority.
- Team costs allocated away from leader production.
- Lifestyle spending obscures business fragility.

## 10. Counterexamples and Confounds

- Deliberate reinvestment can reduce current owner profit.
- Tax/entity choices vary by jurisdiction and owner.

## 17. Evidence, Confidence and Uncertainty

- `CUSTOMER_STATED`: attributed to the customer, not independently verified.
- `DERIVED`: formula or deterministic transformation with inputs/units/formula present.

## 18. Falsifiers

- The hypothesized mechanism is weakened when the named outcome changes while its alleged driver remains unchanged under comparable conditions.
- A competing mechanism explains the same evidence better and survives its own confound tests.
- Source lineage, period, units or identity do not support the claim.
- High execution fidelity produces no movement in the named leading indicators over the stated horizon, absent material confounds.
- New verified evidence contradicts the state contribution.

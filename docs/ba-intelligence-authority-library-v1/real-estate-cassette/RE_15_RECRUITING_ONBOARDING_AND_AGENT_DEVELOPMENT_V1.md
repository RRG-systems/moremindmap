Build a selective recruiting, onboarding and development system that converts appropriate agents into productive, standards-aligned contributors and exits chronic mismatch honestly.

## 5. Causal Mechanisms

- Recruiting volume cannot fix an incoherent team value proposition or unproductive seat.
- Selection quality and expectation clarity affect ramp/retention.
- Onboarding without real work/system/access delays productivity.
- Weak agents can reflect weak lead flow, standards, coaching or role design.
- Retention without productivity can destroy team economics.

## 6. Evidence Inputs

- workforce/constraint plan
- source/candidate funnel
- selection criteria/interviews
- onboarding completion
- time-to-first milestones and production
- lead access/conversion
- coaching/adherence
- retention/exit reasons
- economics

Evidence must retain source identity, date/period, units where relevant, provenance class, confidence, contradictions and missingness. Customer-stated evidence is not automatically verified; model inference is not observation.

## 7. Diagnostic Questions

- What constraint/seat need justifies recruiting?
- Who succeeds here and under what system?
- What does the team promise and prove?
- Which milestones define ramp?
- Is low productivity selection, onboarding, lead flow, skill, fidelity or economics?
- What triggers development versus exit?

## 8. Strong Patterns

- Recruiting tied to business need.
- Selection/ramp/standards are explicit.
- Productivity and retention considered together.

Strong patterns are diagnostic hypotheses supported by evidence, not templates that force every business into the same conclusion.

## 9. Failure Modes

- Agent count is growth metric.
- Recruiting precedes team model/economics.
- Onboarding is orientation only.
- Leader rescue replaces standards.
- Exit criteria are absent.

## 10. Counterexamples and Confounds

- New market/category entrants require longer ramp.
- Low early production may reflect insufficient opportunity allocation.

## 17. Evidence, Confidence and Uncertainty

- `OBSERVED`: directly supported by governed current evidence.
- `CUSTOMER_STATED`: attributed to the customer, not independently verified.
- `DERIVED`: formula or deterministic transformation with inputs/units/formula present.
- `SUPPORTED_HYPOTHESIS`: mechanism fits evidence but alternatives remain.
- `FOUNDER_HEURISTIC`: useful operating doctrine, qualified and parameterized.
- `DYNAMIC_CURRENT`: externally sourced current fact with retrieval date.

## 18. Falsifiers

- The hypothesized mechanism is weakened when the named outcome changes while its alleged driver remains unchanged under comparable conditions.
- A competing mechanism explains the same evidence better and survives its own confound tests.
- Source lineage, period, units or identity do not support the claim.
- High execution fidelity produces no movement in the named leading indicators over the stated horizon, absent material confounds.
- New verified evidence contradicts the state contribution.

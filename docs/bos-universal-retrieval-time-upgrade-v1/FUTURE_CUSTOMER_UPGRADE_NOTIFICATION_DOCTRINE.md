# Future Customer Upgrade Notification Doctrine

Approved core message:

> Your underlying assessment has not changed.<br>
> Our ability to help you understand it has improved.

Any future notification must state that scores, submitted answers, canonical BOS, and Profile ID are unchanged. It may describe clearer language and more explicit uncertainty, but must not imply reassessment, new psychological measurement, diagnosis, increased scientific validation, or a newly observed customer behavior.

Do not implement email, SMS, push, or in-product notification delivery in this campaign.

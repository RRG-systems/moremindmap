# Canonical LO Product Doctrine V1

Authority ID: `loan-originator-product-doctrine-v1`  
Status: canonical product authority; not runtime implementation authority.

## Product subject

LO V1 assesses the business operated by a **producing residential Loan Originator**.

Supported realities may overlap:

- individual producing LO;
- producing LO with team/support leverage;
- retail/direct lender, mortgage broker, and producing bank LO;
- self-generated, company-generated, and blended opportunity models;
- Purchase-heavy, Refinance-heavy, and mixed businesses.

Out of V1 scope: primarily non-producing processor, underwriting, operations, secondary-market, enterprise mortgage-company management, or non-producing corporate leadership roles. Scope failure stops before business evidence acquisition and produces no LO Box 1/downstream projection.

## Product architecture

`confirmed vertical identity → cassette-owned LO intake/evidence → canonical governed BA evidence → cassette-owned LO Box 1 → one Business Twin/WBM → universal Boxes 2–5 → Fusion/BOS → Futures → One Move → Plan → Evidence → one Subscription relationship with governed LO context → future Coach Connect over the same truth`

There is no second WBM, truth store, downstream engine, RSL, mutation engine, or LO-specific coach.

## Customer-experience doctrine

- Twelve core screens and three conditional screens.
- Hard ceiling: 20 customer-facing questions; 20 is not a target.
- Fresh, fully applicable producing path maximum under this contract: 15.
- Evidence-satisfied screens may be skipped only when compatible governed evidence already satisfies definition, period, cohort, subject scope, applicability, freshness, and provenance.
- The intake remains a structured/adaptive assessment, not open-ended coaching chat.
- Use familiar LO language; briefly define MORE-created terms.
- Examples clarify but do not narrow the ontology or imply Realtor primacy.
- Intelligence belongs beneath the question; customer-facing language stays short, obvious, and useful.

## Governing time doctrine

- Primary business baseline: trailing 12 months.
- Current trajectory: trailing 90 days when compatible evidence exists.
- Every material metric carries definition, unit, subject scope, purpose applicability, period, cohort basis, provenance, and evidence class.
- Incompatible periods/cohorts are never mixed for conversion or backsolve arithmetic.
- The two windows live inside LO Box 1/reasoning; they are not a sixth box or a new BA destination.

## Opportunity doctrine

An **Opportunity** is an identifiable person or household with a plausible current mortgage need who entered the LO's business through a professional/business source, Customer Relationship Network, company/direct channel, or self-generated activity.

A relationship, CRM contact, database record, email recipient, social impression, or purchased name without a meaningful mortgage-need signal is not automatically an Opportunity. Source relationship and borrower Opportunity remain distinct objects.

## Relationship-network doctrine

**Opportunity Source Network (OSN):** professional/business relationships and applicable company/direct/digital channels that repeatedly create mortgage Opportunities. Past-customer referrals retain Customer Relationship Network provenance.

**Customer Relationship Network (CRN):** past/current borrowers/customers whose continuing relationship can create repeat Purchase, Refinance, or friends/family referral Opportunities.

Network size alone is not relationship equity. Servicing, recapture, contact/use authority, consent, and data ownership are independent observed/missing states.

## Systems doctrine

**SYSTEMS HAVE NUMBERS. TOOLS ARE NOT SYSTEMS.**

A measurable system establishes who, what, how often, how delivered, owner, and outcome/measure. Relationship-system quality reasons over frequency × channel × relationship depth × audience/segmentation. Automation and personal interaction remain visible. Owning a CRM is not proof of a system.

## Truth doctrine

Evidence classes remain distinct:

- `OBSERVED`
- `DERIVED`
- `MODELED_REQUIREMENT`
- `BENCHMARK`
- `SCENARIO`
- `MISSING`

Question states remain `ANSWERED`, `UNANSWERED`, and explicit governed `NOT_APPLICABLE`. Contradiction is a relation between claims, not a license to average or silently choose. Customer statements carry customer-stated provenance; they do not become independently observed facts.

## Box 1 communication doctrine

Four governed communication states:

- **Opportunity Gap**
- **Opportunity Sufficient / Other Constraint**
- **On Track**
- **Insufficient Evidence**

Recognition before diagnosis. Diagnosis before intervention. Abstention is better than invented intelligence. On Track must not manufacture a problem.

## Constraint doctrine

Eight families:

1. Opportunity Supply
2. Relationship Activation
3. Conversion
4. Pull-Through / Fallout
5. Execution / Systems
6. Capacity / Leverage
7. Platform / Capability
8. Economics / Business Model

One primary constraint appears only when it is the best-supported current limiter on the governed goal. Secondary constraints remain visible. `UNKNOWN / INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE` is a legitimate non-constraint state. Add leverage at the constraint; there is no processor-first rule.

## Founder One Move doctrine

One Move asks:

> What can the person do right now that is most likely to create the greatest positive downstream effects taking them toward a better future?

It may attack a constraint, compound a strength, protect momentum, create leverage, gather decisive evidence, explore a high-value opportunity, or continue what is already working. It is not inherently constraint repair. Evidence gathering is valid only when gaining that evidence has the best expected downstream effect.

## Durable intelligence vs live reality

The cassette stores durable knowledge of how a producing LO business works and how changing context affects it. Current rates, programs, guidelines, conforming limits, market conditions, regulatory changes, incentives, and volatile benchmarks are researched live when materially useful in Subscription/future Coach Connect. External evidence never silently rewrites customer truth.

## Privacy and authority center

The safe center is aggregate business evidence. No borrower names, SSNs, account/loan numbers, exact addresses, income/assets/debts, credit/DTI/LTV, protected-class data, underwriting files, adverse-action reasoning, individualized product/rate recommendation, or raw provider payload is required.

Platform diagnosis does not become Recruiting or employer recommendation authority. Personality may inform communication/adoption/feasibility but never create a business cause.

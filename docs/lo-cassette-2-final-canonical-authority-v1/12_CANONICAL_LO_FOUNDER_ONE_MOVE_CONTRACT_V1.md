# Canonical Founder One Move Contract V1

Authority ID: `loan-originator-founder-one-move-v1`

## Governing definition

> What can the person do **right now** that is most likely to create the greatest positive downstream effects taking them toward a better future?

This later Founder authorization supersedes the earlier narrower statement that One Move should always attack the primary constraint.

## Permitted bases

One Move may:

- attack the best-supported primary constraint;
- compound a strength;
- protect momentum;
- create leverage;
- gather decisive evidence;
- explore a high-value opportunity;
- continue what is already working when continuation has the best expected downstream effect.

It is not inherently repair, hiring, lead generation, processor addition, or a new program.

## Selection contract

A valid One Move should be:

- executable now or immediately startable;
- within the person's legitimate authority/control, or explicitly dependent on another owner;
- grounded in governed current reality and chosen future;
- the smallest credible move with the greatest supported positive downstream effect;
- clear about expected mechanism, evidence, assumptions, and uncertainty;
- measurable/falsifiable over an appropriate horizon;
- privacy/compliance safe;
- compatible with current capacity and platform reality;
- revisable when outcomes disagree.

## State-specific behavior

- **Opportunity Gap:** may target supply/activation/conversion, but only the best-supported move.
- **Opportunity Sufficient / Other Constraint:** target the supported limiter or a higher-effect strength/leverage opportunity.
- **On Track:** do not manufacture a repair. A continuation/protection/compounding move may be best; abstention remains legitimate if no discrete move improves the future.
- **Insufficient Evidence:** choose an evidence move only when that evidence has the best expected downstream effect. Otherwise abstain or pursue a supported non-diagnostic move.

## Universal coaching integration

One Move remains a universal downstream mission. LO cassette supplies governed mechanics, evidence, constraints, assets, and authority boundaries. The cassette does not select a deterministic LO move or conversation path.

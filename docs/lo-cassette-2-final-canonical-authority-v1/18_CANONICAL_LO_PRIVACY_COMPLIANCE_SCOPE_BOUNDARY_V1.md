# Canonical LO Privacy, Compliance, and Scope Boundary Contract V1

Authority ID: `loan-originator-privacy-compliance-scope-v1`

## V1 assessed subject

The producing residential Loan Originator's aggregate business. Non-producing operations/corporate roles stop at scope and produce no LO projection.

## Required safe evidence

- aggregate counts/volume/ratios;
- purpose/source segments;
- periods/cohorts/definitions;
- system activity/outcomes;
- team/work ownership/capacity;
- platform capability/control owner;
- business economics under explicit scope;
- goals/model assumptions;
- evidence provenance/missingness/contradiction.

## Explicitly not required

- borrower/customer names;
- SSNs, account/loan numbers, exact addresses;
- income, assets, debts, credit, DTI, LTV;
- race, ethnicity, sex, age, disability, family status, or any protected-class data/inference;
- underwriting files, adverse-action reasons, qualification/eligibility decisions;
- raw borrower communications;
- individualized rate/product/loan recommendations;
- servicing files or assumed recapture/contact rights;
- referral/compensation-law conclusions;
- provider payloads, secrets, or real customer retrieval.

If sensitive borrower-level data is offered, reject ingestion and request the minimum aggregate substitute.

## Contact/use/servicing/recapture boundary

Each remains an independent observed/missing authority state. Originating a loan, possessing a CRM row, or serving a customer historically does not prove present contact/use, data ownership, servicing, or recapture authority.

No intake output itself authorizes outreach, marketing, referral compensation, product recommendation, data transfer, or servicing/recapture action. Applicable company/compliance/legal review remains necessary.

## Business-funnel versus underwriting

Qualified/Preapproved/Approval are declared aggregate company business events. MORE does not perform borrower qualification, underwriting, fair-lending analysis, steering, or eligibility inference.

## External/current reality

Rates, programs, guidelines, conforming limits, laws/regulations, market conditions, incentives, platform policies, and benchmarks are volatile dated external evidence. Research only when materially useful and authorized in Subscription/future Coach Connect. They do not enter durable customer truth automatically.

## Platform/Recruiting boundary

The cassette may recognize a platform capability constraint and control owner. It may not recommend an employer, compare recruiting opportunities, infer dissatisfaction, or generate recruiting strategy. Recruiting remains separately governed.

## BOS boundary

Personality may affect communication/adoption/feasibility but cannot establish business cause, vulnerability, financial need, or suitability.

## Runtime/Production boundary

This canonical authority freeze changes no Real Estate intake, registry, runtime, schema, persistence, WBM, Box 1, Subscription, Coach Connect, customer state, provider, secret, deployment, or Production source.

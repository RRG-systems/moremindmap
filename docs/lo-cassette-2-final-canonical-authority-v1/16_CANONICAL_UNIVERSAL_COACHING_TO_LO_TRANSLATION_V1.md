## Universal principles requiring LO translation

### Goal/opportunity-flow backsolving

Translate the chosen future through LO-specific definitions/rates/capacity/economics. All requirements remain modeled with visible sensitivity.

### Opportunity sufficiency

Use the LO Opportunity definition, mature compatible cohort, purpose segmentation, and feasibility. “Enough leads” is not a mandatory first question or deterministic branch.

### Capacity/leverage gating

If flow appears sufficient, validate conversion, sustainability, economics, concentration, platform, and evidence before scale. Add leverage at the supported constraint; no processor-first rule.

### Durable relationship/demand assets

Translate through OSN and CRN without treating contact count, CRM ownership, Realtor primacy, servicing, or recapture as given.

### Four Laws of Lead Generation

Canonical neutral abstraction:

`asset → replenishment → nurture → opportunity stewardship`

The label may remain available as Founder Seed, but LO mechanics use OSN/CRN/purpose/funnel evidence rather than Real Estate stages.

### Four Laws of Business

Use big vision, fit-for-purpose organization, builder roles, and adaptive persistence only where subject scope and evidence support them. No automatic hiring sequence.

## Portable optional heuristics

6×6/8×8, 25+/35+ Touch, 4–1–1, and Win-the-Day/Week/Month/Year are optional tools. Each requires:

- applicable audience/problem;
- owner;
- evidence window;
- measure/outcome;
- stop/adapt condition;
- consent/company/compliance fit;
- no mandatory workflow or score.

## BOS/Fusion boundary

Whole-Person evidence may inform communication, adoption, feasibility, pressure response, or plan design. It cannot create the source, conversion, capacity, platform, economics, or other business cause. BOS `Perspective` and business `Perspective` remain separate namespaces.

## Free-the-GPT boundary

The authority provides knowledge, meaning, constraints, provenance, uncertainty, and safe actions. The frontier coach decides what matters, what to ask, what to explain, what to research, and how to speak naturally. No preselected lens, scripted LO conversation, question tree, reveal cadence, or deterministic coach is created.

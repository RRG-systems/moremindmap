# Canonical LO Downstream Governed Projection Contract V1

Route ID: `loan-originator-whole-business-route-v1`  
Subscription context ID: `loan-originator-subscription-context-v1`  
Evidence routing dependency: `loan-originator-business-evidence-routing-v1`  
This document specifies canonical meaning, not runtime implementation.

## Single projection path

`LO intake evidence → canonical BA evidence → one Business Twin/WBM → cassette-owned LO Box 1 → universal Boxes 2–5 → BOS/BA Fusion → Futures → One Move → 1–3–5 Plan → Evidence → Subscription governed LO context → future Coach Connect`

## Canonical LO evidence packet

The canonical pure packet contains:

- vertical/binding/subject scope and supported variant flags;
- primary goal;
- 12-month baseline and 90-day trajectory;
- Purchase/Refinance applicability/mix;
- OSN and CRN as separate objects;
- purpose-specific funnel definitions/counts/cohorts/derived ratios;
- conversion/fallout/cycle explanation only when governed;
- economics/backsolve and sensitivity when applicable;
- relationship/operating systems;
- team/capacity/work ownership;
- platform/company capability and control owner;
- operator hypothesis;
- accountability/execution/outcome/learning evidence;
- sufficiency state;
- primary/secondary constraint or abstention;
- five Box 1 projections;
- evidence classes, provenance, confidence, applicability, missingness, contradiction, definitions, periods/cohorts;
- current external research references, never merged into customer truth.

## Consumer-by-consumer contract

### Canonical BA evidence

Stores governed LO evidence under cassette authority. It preserves New BA question states/missingness and does not translate Real Estate q1–q12.

### Business Twin / WBM

Receives normalized LO relationships, mechanisms, constraints, and evidence through the existing one-model architecture. No second LO WBM or Real Estate fallback.

### Box 1

Cassette-owned five surfaces. Full/narrow/contradictory/abstain/On Track/out-of-scope behavior is explicit.

The visible surface identity is fixed and ordered:

1. **Your Business Now**
2. **Where Your Business Comes From**
3. **Your Business Pipeline**
4. **Your System & Capacity**
5. **What’s Holding You Back**

OSN and CRN project as visibly separate governed portfolios within Where Your Business Comes From. Measured systems, work ownership, team/support, capacity, and material platform constraints remain combined within Your System & Capacity without erasing their typed distinctions. These labels and mappings do not alter the canonical evidence packet, Business Twin/WBM, universal Boxes 2–5, or any downstream consumer contract.

### Universal Boxes 2–5

Consume governed vertical content through unchanged missions. They may not repair missing evidence or inherit LO UI mechanics.

### Fusion/BOS

Uses Whole Person for communication/adoption/feasibility only. Personality never manufactures business causality; Perspective namespaces stay distinct.

### Futures

Builds supported alternative business futures using governed evidence/assumptions. No product/rate/underwriting recommendation and no hidden missingness repair.

### One Move

Uses the Founder definition and may target constraint, strength, momentum, leverage, evidence, opportunity, or continuation. No forced move when unsupported.

### Plan

Existing governed 1–3–5. Uses safe aggregate actions, owners, measures, outcomes, and stop/adapt conditions. No filler or platform-control assumption.

### Evidence

Requests/validates safe aggregate proof and preserves truth class, provenance, definitions, periods, and contradictions. Benchmarks/scenarios never become observed.

### Subscription governed LO context

Receives the whole coaching-relevant governed packet plus Business Twin, Futures, One Move, Plan, BOS/Whole Person, Personal RSL, and important provenance. The one frontier coach decides what matters; intake order is not conversation choreography.

No LO-specific session runtime, script, lens router, second RSL/mutation engine/truth store, or deterministic update flow.

### Future Coach Connect

Uses the same person/business/relationship truth plus authorized human-coach identity and typed coach evidence. The customer continues normal Subscription during coaching. The coach receives the Subscription/business intelligence plus a simple coach workspace/panel, notes, accountability/commitments, and report surfaces. Coach notes have extraordinary evidentiary importance while preserving observation/hypothesis/customer-confirmed/commitment/outcome types.

## Recruiting boundary

Platform/capability findings remain business evidence and control-owner context. They cannot create employer comparison, recruiting angle, or recommendation. Recruiting consumes only separately governed authority.

- no data mutation or side effect;
- no inferred vertical/Real Estate fallback;
- explicit unsupported/out-of-scope failure;
- field-level lineage retained;
- consumer-specific minimization without semantic loss;

# Unresolved gaps and bounded terminal verdict

## What is proven

- A real frontier model, not a deterministic dialogue router, chooses substantive relevance, wording, hypotheses, and representation.
- YOU has two independent BOS-only protections: public world scoping and private context construction. BA is not merely hidden; it is not requested.
- YOUR BUSINESS receives the intended whole-person, whole-business, first-party, doctrine, domain, evidence, and conversation context.
- Protected first-party answers can materially improve the abstraction without becoming public evidence or raw dialogue.
- The system can present one useful idea and one question while retaining whole-state understanding underneath.
- Human disagreement changes the model's working understanding and preserves supersession lineage.
- GU is not limited to a dashboard or single chart family.
- Progressive technical rendering, CAS/state binding, evidence references, action allowlists, missingness, privacy validation, and canonical non-mutation remain intact.

## Unresolved but bounded

1. **Real canonical execution was not exercised with customer data.** The legitimate BOS/BA read-only path is traced and wired. Source failures produce exact unavailable receipts. Exercising a real profile was outside this synthetic-only authorization.
2. **Latency is usable for an experiment, not yet invisible.** The sealed mean was 9.537 seconds, with an exploratory outlier near 41 seconds. Immediate progress states keep the surface alive, but tail latency still needs product work.
3. **Strict validation still rejects some frontier plans.** This is safer than rendering invented or incompatible content, but the browser observed one invalid BUSINESS plan before a clean fresh-session success. Future work should improve model adherence or safe repair efficiency, not relax truth/privacy validation.
4. **Privacy validation is a hard backstop, not a proof of perfect semantic privacy.** Verbatim windows and explicit sensitive terms are denied, while the prompt directs abstraction. A broader adversarial privacy evaluation belongs before live exposure.
5. **This experiment does not authorize Production readiness.** Load, provider failure UX, operational observability, real canonical fixtures, accessibility, and promotion reconciliation remain separate gates.

## Blunt verdict

**BOUNDED EXPERIMENT PASS — FREE FRONTIER COACHING MERITS CONTINUED DEVELOPMENT; NOT AUTHORIZED FOR PRODUCTION OR DARREN DEMO.**

MORE felt meaningfully more like a thinking partner than an analyst dumping a dossier. The strongest evidence is not stylistic: the model used a protected source detail to improve its internal frame, refused to expose the detail, revised when corrected, and chose different visual primitives for different purposes. The ball moved.

The experiment does not prove that every frontier turn will be fast, valid, or perfectly calibrated. It proves the architecture can support whole-state intelligence underneath and progressive human understanding above without replacing governance with choreography.
